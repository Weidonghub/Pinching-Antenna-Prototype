System Architecture

系统架构

An end-to-end wireless video communication prototype integrating FPGA-based CP-OFDM signal processing, AD9361 transceivers, 60 GHz frequency conversion, a dielectric waveguide, and a pinching antenna.

该原型构建了一套端到端无线视频通信链路，集成 FPGA CP-OFDM 信号处理、AD9361 收发机、60 GHz 频率变换、 介质波导以及夹持天线。

01

Baseband Processing

基带处理

The video data stream is processed on the FPGA platform using CP-OFDM baseband signal processing.

视频数据流在 FPGA 平台完成基于 CP-OFDM 的基带信号处理。

→
02

IF Processing & 60 GHz Upconversion

中频处理与 60 GHz 上变频

The signal is processed through the AD9361 transceiver and up-converted to the 60 GHz millimeter-wave band.

信号经 AD9361 完成中频收发处理，并进一步 上变频至 60 GHz 毫米波频段。

→
03

Waveguide & Pinching Antenna

介质波导与夹持天线

The 60 GHz signal is fed into the dielectric waveguide and coupled out into free space through the pinching antenna.

60 GHz 信号注入介质波导，并通过夹持天线 将导波能量耦合并辐射到自由空间。

→
04

Receive & Video Recovery

接收与视频恢复

A horn antenna receives the 60 GHz RF signal, which is then down-converted and processed by the AD9361 and FPGA to recover the video stream for display on a laptop.

接收端采用喇叭天线接收 60 GHz 射频信号， 经下变频后由 AD9361 和 FPGA 完成接收处理， 恢复视频数据并在电脑上显示。
